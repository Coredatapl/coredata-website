# Security Policy

Effective as of: **20.01.2026**

---

## Introduction

This document outlines basic security practices for our organization, focusing on vulnerability reporting, data protection, and incident response. It applies to all employees, contractors, and users interacting with our systems.

## Definitions

Company
: (referred to as either "the Company", "We", "Us" or "Our" in this Agreement) refers to Coredata, Wilanów, Warsaw Masovian district Poland, PL.

Website
: refers to Coredata website, accessible from [https://coredata.pl](https://coredata.pl)

Service
: refers to any service or application developed and provided by the Company including the Website

## Vulnerability Reporting

If you discover a potential security vulnerability such as software bugs, misconfigurations, or unauthorized access in the Service, **report it immediately** using our secure channel.

- **Method**: Send details via email to [hello&#64;coredata.pl](hello@coredata.pl).

- What to include:
  - Description of the vulnerability.
  - Steps to reproduce it.
  - Affected services or versions.
  - Your contact information (optional, for follow-up).
  - If you have a fix, that is most welcome - please attach or summarize it in your message.

- We will evaluate the vulnerability and, if necessary, release a fix or mitigating steps to address it. We will contact you to let you know the outcome, and will credit you in the report.

Please **do not disclose the vulnerability publicly** until a fix is released!

Once we have either published a fix, or declined to address the vulnerability for whatever reason, you are free to publicly disclose it.

## Access Control

Please follow these recommendations:

- Use strong, unique passwords (at least 12 characters, including uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols).
- Enable multi-factor authentication (MFA) when available.
- Limit access to the principle of least privilege.

## Data Protection

Please follow these recommendations:

- Encrypt sensitive data in transit and at rest.
- Avoid sharing credentials or sensitive information via unsecured channels.
- Regularly back up critical data to secure, offsite locations.

## Review and Updates

This policy is reviewed annually or after major incidents. Last updated: **January 20, 2026**.
